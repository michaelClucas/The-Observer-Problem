## 8. Honest notes on today's own reliability

the same hazards apply to a day's work.

**Two near-misses that would have published wrong conclusions**, both in
WP-3's cycle 4, both recorded in the day log rather than tidied away:

1. The curvature matrix was first built in raw amplitudes whose per-index
   steps span thirty orders of magnitude. Condition number around `1e60`;
   the output was arithmetic noise, announcing itself as `|rho| > 1` and
   non-positive-definite matrices, both impossible.
2. Then the response step was normalised at the regularisation scale rather
   than at the pivot, which made the observable effect unmeasurable for the
   steep branches and returned **every** pair as "degenerate". **That would
   have been reported as a D3 or D4 verdict on the plan's centrepiece, and
   it was an artefact of a normalisation choice.**

The guard that caught both is that `|rho| <= 1` and positive-definiteness
are checkable properties of the answer, independent of the physics. The
script now checks them and refuses to report rather than returning a
number. **The generalisable lesson: a statistic with a known mathematical
constraint should have that constraint asserted in the code, because it is
the only thing standing between a conditioning bug and a false headline.**
